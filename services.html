Detailed Testing Services

We provide accurate and reliable testing following IS standards to ensure quality and safety in construction.

Soil Testing

Detailed soil investigation for foundation safety and stability analysis.

  • Grain Size Analysis (Sieve Analysis)
  • Atterberg Limits (Liquid Limit, Plastic Limit)
  • Specific Gravity
  • Proctor Compaction Test
  • California Bearing Ratio (CBR)
  • Direct Shear Test
  • Triaxial Shear Test

Sand Testing

Quality analysis of fine aggregates to ensure suitability for construction.

  • Sieve Analysis
  • Silt Content Test
  • Bulking of Sand
  • Specific Gravity
  • Moisture Content
  • Organic Impurities Test

Concrete Testing

Strength and quality testing of concrete for structural integrity.

  • Compressive Strength Test
  • Slump Test
  • Cube Testing
  • Flexural Strength
  • Water Absorption
  • Rebound Hammer Test

Aggregate Testing

Durability testing of coarse aggregates for long-term performance.

  • Crushing Value Test
  • Impact Value Test
  • Abrasion Value (Los Angeles)
  • Flakiness and Elongation Index
  • Specific Gravity
  • Water Absorption
  • Soundness Test

Water Testing

Chemical testing of construction water to meet quality standards.

  • pH Value
  • Hardness
  • Chloride Content
  • Sulphate Content
  • Alkalinity
  • Organic Matter
  • Turbidity
