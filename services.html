Complete Civil Engineering & Construction Services

We offer full-spectrum services – accurate material testing, expert design & consultancy, and reliable construction delivery – tailored for residential, commercial, institutional and infrastructure projects in Assam and the North East.

Cement Testing

Full quality testing as per IS standards.

  • (i) Normal Consistency
  • (ii) Initial & Final Setting Time
  • (iii) Specific Gravity
  • (iv) Fineness (Dry Sieving)
  • Fineness (Air Permeability)
  • (v) Soundness
  • (vi) Compressive Strength (3 cubes)

Fine Aggregate Testing

Quality checks for mortar and concrete works.

  • (i) Particle Size Distribution
  • (ii) Specific Gravity
  • (iii) Water Absorption
  • (iv) Bulking of Sand
  • (v) Relative Density
  • (vi) Silt Content
  • (vii) Presence of Deleterious Materials

Coarse Aggregate Testing

Durability & strength properties testing.

  • (i) Particle Size Distribution
  • (ii) Specific Gravity
  • (iii) Water Absorption
  • (iv) Bulk Density & voids
  • (v) Crushing Value
  • (vi) Impact Value
  • (vii) Presence of Deleterious Materials
  • (viii) Abrasion Value
  • (ix) Flakiness Index
  • (x) Elongation Index
  • (xi) Floor Tiles Abrasion Test

Brick Testing

Quality assessment of clay & fly ash bricks.

  • (i) Dimension Test
  • (ii) Water Absorption Test
  • (iii) Efflorescence Test
  • (iv) Compressive Strength

Tests on Steel (Per Diameter, per number)

Mechanical & chemical compliance check.

  • (i) Ultimate Tensile Strength
  • (ii) Bend & Re-bend Test
  • (iii) Elongation Test
  • (iv) Unit Weight

Tests on Soil (Per Sample)

Foundation recommendation and site suitability studies.

  • (i) CBR (in Lab)
  • (ii) OMC & MDD
  • (iii) Sieve Analysis
  • (iv) Specific Gravity
  • (v) Liquid Limit & Plastic Limit
  • (vi) Field Density (Lab)
  • (vii) Field SPT

Concrete Testing

Fresh & hardened concrete quality verification.

  • (i) Concrete Cube (3 nos.)
  • (ii) Concrete Mix Design (Up to M25)
  • (ii) Concrete Mix Design (Above M25)
  • (iii) Compressive Strength Test of Cement Concrete Paver Block

Bitumen Testing

Performance grading for roads & waterproofing.

  • (i) Softening Point
  • (ii) Viscosity Test
  • (iii) Specific Gravity
  • (iv) Boiling Point
  • (v) Flash Point
  • (vi) Marshall Stability
