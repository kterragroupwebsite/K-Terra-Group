Our Services

Comprehensive civil engineering solutions from design to construction

Soil Testing

Detailed soil investigation including CBR, OMC & MDD, sieve analysis, specific gravity, liquid limit, plastic limit, and field density tests for safe and stable foundation design.

Construction Material Testing

Comprehensive testing of construction materials including cement, sand, aggregate, concrete, brick, steel, and bitumen as per IS standards to ensure quality, durability, and safety.

Design and Drawing

Expert architectural and structural design services with detailed technical drawings, blueprints, and CAD models for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

Consultancy and Construction

Professional civil engineering consultancy, project planning, feasibility studies, construction supervision, quality assurance, and turnkey project execution from concept to completion.
