Our Story

Founded with a passion for precision and safety in construction.

K Terra Group was established over a decade ago in Tezpur, Assam, with the vision of providing top-notch civil testing services to the construction industry. Starting as a small laboratory, we have grown into a trusted name, serving engineers, contractors, and builders across the region.

Our commitment to accuracy, reliability, and adherence to international standards has been the cornerstone of our success. We specialize in soil, sand, concrete, aggregate, and water testing, ensuring every project we touch meets the highest quality benchmarks.
