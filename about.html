About Kterra North East Infra

Building trust, resilience, and a safer future through quality civil engineering solutions in North East India.

Who We Are

A forward-thinking civil engineering company based in Tezpur, Assam, committed to transforming construction practices across the North East.

Rekib Ahmed

Founder & Managing Director

With a strong vision for safe, innovative, and accessible construction, Rekib Ahmed leads Kterra North East Infra in delivering end-to-end civil engineering solutions. Passionate about promoting scientific construction practices in seismically active North East India, he drives the company's focus on quality material testing, structural integrity, and affordable services for individual homeowners.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, innovative, and accessible civil engineering solutions while promoting safety and quality construction practices across North East India.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and comprehensive civil engineering solutions provider in the region, setting new standards in quality, safety, and innovation.

Our Commitment

Safety, quality, transparency, and continuous improvement are at the heart of everything we do.

Kterra North East Infra is dedicated to making essential services like soil investigation and material testing affordable and accessible — especially for individual house owners — while fostering innovation through research, internships, and training for aspiring engineers.
